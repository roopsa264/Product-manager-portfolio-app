About me

I’m an explorer at heart. When something catches my interest, I go deep: I read the articles, study the user stories, and keep asking why it works and how it was made. That habit has left me with a head full of research and random facts, and I’ve always loved sharing what I find with the people around me.

What I love even more is creating and deploying. I enjoy driving numbers, understanding the business and what users actually need, and finding the bridge between the two. As a product manager I’m also the bridge between teams, so stakeholder management is a big part of how I work. My B.Tech background lets me build with intent and guide engineers better, while my creative side keeps the ideas coming.

To me, product management is storytelling. You empathise with what users need and want, then deliver it. Bringing a product to life, from imagining its design and functions to watching people use it, is the part I enjoy most.
